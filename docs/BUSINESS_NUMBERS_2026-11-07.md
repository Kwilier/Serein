## 1. Cost of the problem

**Scale (children 0-17, US)**
- 374,265 pediatric asthma ED visits (51.4 per 10,000 children); 60,770 inpatient stays; 166 deaths. CDC Most Recent Asthma Data, 2023 data. https://www.cdc.gov/asthma-data/about/most-recent-asthma-data.html
- Derived: 374,265 / 4,804,000 = about 78 ED visits and 12.6 hospitalizations per 1,000 children with asthma per year (our arithmetic from CDC 2023).
- 628,759 Medicaid/CHIP-paid pediatric asthma ED visits, $272.5M; CA 58,200 visits / $25.2M; OR 6,427 / $2.8M; WA 8,531 / $3.7M. Pearson et al., CDC Preventing Chronic Disease, 2014 (2010 data). https://cdc.gov/pcd/issues/2014/14_0139.htm

**Unit costs**
- $433 mean Medicaid payment per pediatric asthma ED visit (2011 claims). Pearson et al., PCD 2014. Same URL.
- $440 mean hospital cost per pediatric treat-and-release ED visit, all causes (2021). HCUP Statistical Brief 311. https://hcup-us.ahrq.gov/reports/statbriefs/sb311-ED-visit-costs-2021.pdf
- $7,464 mean hospital cost per pediatric asthma hospitalization (2022 USD); 64,393 stays; $480.6M national. Gabbay et al., Annals ATS 22(10):1611, Oct 2025. https://academic.oup.com/annalsats/article/22/10/1611/8444111
- $82B/yr total US asthma cost, all ages (2008-2013); $3,266/yr extra medical cost per person with asthma. Nurmagambetov et al. 2018 via AAFA Facts and Figures (Apr 2026). https://aafa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/aafa-asthma-facts-and-figures.pdf (AAFA's "$118B in 2026" is its own inflation adjustment: FLAG)

**Wildfire smoke and pediatric asthma visits**
- +30% pediatric respiratory ED/urgent-care visits per 10-unit increase in wildfire PM2.5 vs +3.7% for non-wildfire PM2.5; San Diego 2011-2017. Aguilera et al., Pediatrics 2021. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33757996/ (via STAT https://www.statnews.com/2021/03/23/wildfire-smoke-is-particularly-harmful-to-kids-respiratory-health-study-finds/ ; unit "10 µg/m³" FLAG: confirm in the paper)
- Respiratory ED visits rise 30-110% the week after an extreme smoke day; about 3,010 excess CA ED visits/yr, all causes, all ages. Heft-Neal et al., PNAS 2023. https://stanford.edu/~samhn/papers/Heft-Neal%20et%20al%202023%20-%20PNAS.pdf
- Washington, all ages, 2014-2018 fire seasons: asthma ED visits +4% (Moderate), +8% (USG), +12% (Unhealthy) vs no-smoke days; 609 excess ED visits; $4.5M excess cost. WA Office of Financial Management. https://ofm.wa.gov/sites/default/files/public/dataresearch/healthcare/pdf/smoke_exposure_extract.pdf
- Oregon: asthma-like ED visits about +25% statewide during the 2020 Labor Day fires; 2022 respiratory ED visits +44% (Washington Co.), +35% (Clackamas). OHA, Nov 12, 2025. https://www.oregon.gov/oha/erd/pages/oha-data-show-poor-air-quality-linked-to-more-emergency-visits.aspx
- Children 0-4: +8.3% asthma admissions per 10 µg/m³ PM2.5, 2003 SoCal fires. Delfino et al. 2009 via Frontiers in Pediatrics 2022. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pediatrics/articles/10.3389/fped.2022.891616/full
- +27% asthma ED visits during the 14-day Camp Fire smoke wave, Bay Area. Komodo Health brief, Aug 2024 (industry white paper, not peer-reviewed: FLAG). https://knowledge.komodohealth.com/hubfs/_2024/Komodo_Health_Asthma_and_Wildfires_Research_Brief_Aug_2024.pdf
- No published state-level, pediatric, smoke-attributable asthma visit count exists for CA/OR/WA. Any such number is our own derivation; say so.

## 6. Price range and break-even (reasoning for Brief Q9)

Anchors: SchoolMessenger ~$1.90/student; ParentSquare ~$4.50/student and one $69,913 contract; Raptor $2,095/building; sensor subscriptions $500-$5,000/unit-yr.

Positioning: a single-purpose safety layer, priced below a full comms platform and at or below one Raptor building license:
- Districts: $500-$1,000 per building per year, or $0.50-$1.50 per student, $1,500 floor. 10,000-student district: $5,000-$15,000/yr.
- Health plans: $10-$20 per enrolled child with asthma per year, under one Medi-Cal 30-minute education unit ($26.66).

Break-even per 1,000 children with asthma: baseline about 78 ED visits ($440 each) and 12.6 hospitalizations ($7,464 each), roughly $128k/yr in hospital costs. At $5/child/yr, break-even is 11 avoided ED visits (15% of baseline) or 0.7 avoided hospitalizations (5%). At $15/child/yr: 34 ED visits or 2 hospitalizations.

Reality check: smoke-attributable visits are plausibly 1-5% of annual pediatric asthma ED visits in a smoky year, so an ED-only argument supports about $0.50-$2 per child; the hospitalization channel and non-hospital value (missed school, parent work loss, plan quality metrics) justify $10-$20. The strongest honest line: one avoided hospitalization funds 370-750 child-years of Serein. We have no evidence yet that Serein avoids visits; a pilot would measure it.

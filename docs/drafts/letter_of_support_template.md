# Letter of support template (task E-7)

Ask a school nurse, district health coordinator, clinic, asthma coalition, or air
district staff member who has seen the demo. Offer to draft it for them (most will
say yes); they edit and sign. Keep it to one page. A letter that says "we would
pilot this" is worth more than one that says "this is a good idea."

Attach as one of the two optional Innovation Brief attachments or quote from it in
Q5 and Q8.

---

[Organization letterhead or name]
[Date]

To the Conrad Challenge judges,

I am the [title] at [organization], where I [one sentence on what they do on smoke
days: e.g., "oversee health services for 12 schools and about 9,000 students, roughly
600 of whom have asthma on file"].

On wildfire smoke days, my job is to [decide whether recess and PE move indoors /
advise families / field calls from parents]. Today I rely on [AirNow / the county air
district / PurpleAir / news], which gives me [a forecast for the whole metro area,
usually the same morning]. [One honest sentence about what goes wrong: "Smoke has
arrived before our forecast changed more than once, and I have no way to know
which of our schools will be hit first."]

I met the Serein team on [date] and reviewed their neighborhood smoke forecast and the
plan it generates for an individual child. [One or two specific sentences in their own
words about what they found useful or credible, e.g., "The one-to-three day lead time
and the per-school map would let me plan PE schedules the day before instead of at
7 a.m.," or "I appreciated that the plans can only be stricter than EPA guidance."]

[Pick one:]
- If the team makes a free version available for the [2027] fire season, [organization]
  would be willing to pilot it with [N] schools and give the team feedback.
- I would be willing to review their family messages before any family receives them.
- I support the team's application and would be glad to speak with judges about how
  our district handles smoke days.

This letter reflects my own professional view and is not a procurement commitment
by [organization].

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Title, organization]
[Email or phone, optional]

---

## Notes for the team

- Only true statements. If they have not seen the demo, show them first.
- Ask whether their organization needs approval before signing (some districts do;
  the last paragraph is there to make signing easier).
- Save a PDF in `docs/letters/` (git-ignored if it has a signature or contact info);
  reference it in the brief by organization type only if they prefer.

**Subject:** Student research project: request for historical sensor data access

Hello PurpleAir team,

We are a team of five high school students building *Serein*, a wildfire-smoke
forecast for families of children with asthma, for the 2026-27 Conrad Challenge.

tested on past fire seasons it never trained on.

We already run the full study on free EPA monitors. PurpleAir's network would let
us test whether dense low-cost sensors improve neighborhood-level forecasts, which

outdoor sensors in Sacramento and Portland, June-October 2021-2025. By our estimate
that is roughly 5.5 million hourly rows. We'd apply the EPA correction and A/B QC, credit
PurpleAir in all materials, and share our findings with you.
